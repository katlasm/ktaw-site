This policy explains what information the Muscle Debt Score assessment collects, why, and what happens to it. It covers this specific tool; Kettlebellin': The Atlas Way's own checkout and program delivery are handled separately by ThriveCart and the KTAW Hub, which have their own privacy practices.

What we collect

When you take the Muscle Debt Score assessment, we collect:

  • Your answers to the assessment questions — sex, age, weight figures, timeframes, training habits, protein intake, sleep, and (depending on your path) medication type, surgical recovery details, or activity restriction level
  • Answers to the medical screening question, if shown (e.g., pregnancy, recent fainting or dizziness, eating disorder history, doctor-restricted activity)
  • Your first name and email address, provided before your results are shown

We do not collect payment information through this tool — purchases of Kettlebellin': The Atlas Way are handled entirely by ThriveCart, a separate service with its own privacy and security practices.

How we use it

  • To calculate your estimated Muscle Debt Score and generate your personalized results
  • To send you your results by email, and follow-up emails related to the assessment and Kettlebellin': The Atlas Way
  • To route you appropriately if your answers indicate a medical consideration worth addressing with a doctor first — we do not diagnose or make medical decisions; screening answers are used only to adjust what we recommend next

What this assessment is not

Your Muscle Debt Score is an estimate based on your inputs and published research on weight loss and muscle loss. It is not a medical diagnosis, not a substitute for professional medical advice, and not a body composition scan. If you have a medical condition or concerns about starting a new training program, talk to a physician first.

Who your information is shared with

Your name, email, and assessment answers are stored in our email platform (Kit) to deliver your results and any follow-up communication. We do not sell your information to third parties. If you purchase Kettlebellin': The Atlas Way, your purchase is processed by ThriveCart, and program delivery happens through the KTAW Hub — both receive only what's needed to fulfill your order and give you access.

How long we keep it

We retain your information for as long as you remain subscribed to our email communications, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

  • Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information at any time by contacting us (see below)

Cookies and local storage

This site uses your browser's local storage to remember your place in the assessment. It does not use third-party tracking cookies as part of the assessment itself.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: support@mymuscledebt.com
